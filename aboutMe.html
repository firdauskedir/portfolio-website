ABOUT ME

Curious about technology,

I’m Firdaus, a Software Engineering student at Addis Ababa University with a growing focus on frontend development and UI/UX design.

I enjoy turning ideas into clean, responsive interfaces that are easy to use. My foundation is in HTML, CSS and JavaScript, which spread out to React.js and I’m continuing to build my skills through hands-on projects rather than only studying theory.

I’m currently working on improving my skills in React.js and expanding my understanding of modern frontend development and exploring backend technologies. I am also developing my skills in networking and database management using PostgreSQL. I’m looking for an internship where I can contribute, learn from experienced developers and gain real-world experience.
